Detective Paws Privacy Policy

Pawz Gamez · Last updated: 14 November 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Detective Paws ("the game"), made by Pawz Gamez ("we"), collects and how it is used. We have kept the game as private as we can: it has no accounts, no sign-in and no chat.

Information the game stores on your device

  • Your game progress: solved cases, stars, coins, outfits and sound settings.
  • This information is saved only on your phone. We never receive it, and it is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

Character voices

The character voices use your phone's built-in text-to-speech engine. The text is spoken on your device, and we do not record or collect any audio. The game does not use your microphone.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and is supported by ads provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • Your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers
  • Approximate location, based on your IP address
  • Device and app information, such as phone model, operating system and language
  • How you interact with ads, and diagnostic and performance data

This data is collected and handled by Google under Google's Privacy Policy. You can learn more about how Google uses information from apps at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Players in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google's consent message. You can reset your advertising ID or turn off ad personalisation at any time in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Rewarded ads

Some features, such as extra hints, more time or bonus coins, are offered in exchange for watching an ad. Watching these ads is always optional.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through the game, please contact us and we will help remove it.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information. Apart from the advertising data handled by Google AdMob described above, the game does not share information with anyone.

Security

Ad data is sent to Google over encrypted connections. Game progress never leaves your device.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game changes. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.
